# Review drills R1–R5

Five short files with problems in them. R1–R4 are practice: you get feedback, not a score. **R5 (Day 9) is recorded and scored**: screen recording on, think aloud, recording link in your EOD email.

On the day your plan names, take **20 minutes with a timer, no AI tools, no IDE help** (a plain editor is fine):

1. List every issue: what it is, why it matters, and the fix.
2. Rank the issues from most to least serious, and say why the top one is first.
3. Write your answer in `review-drills/answers/R<N>.md`.

You review, you don't run. R4 is a JMeter test plan (`.jmx`, read the XML); the others are Playwright tests written for Toolshop.
Rule of thumb for ranking: a test that passes while the feature is broken is worse than a test that is slow or messy.
