# Day 5 scenario — your automation plan

You start next Monday as the only QA on **Toolshop** (the practice app you use in this plan), placed by Full Scale.

- The client has **300 manual regression cases** in a spreadsheet. A full manual regression takes the team 3 days.
- They release **every 2 weeks**.
- You also test **6 to 10 sprint tickets** each sprint.
- There is no automation and no CI for tests. The developers use GitHub Actions for builds.
- The client lead asks: "In 6 weeks, what will be automated, and how will I know each week that it's on track?"

Write `docs/automation-plan.md` (one page): week-by-week milestones for 6 weeks, how you split your time with sprint tickets (and how you'll agree it with the client), what you'll leave manual and why, and the risks you see now.
